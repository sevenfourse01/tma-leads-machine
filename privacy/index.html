4. Enquiry assessments of dental practices (Mercer)

What it is. We assess how a dental practice handles a routine new-patient enquiry: whether it is acknowledged, how long a reply takes and whether the question is answered. An enquiry is sent by a fictional enquirer (a "persona") and is always routine and non-clinical. A persona never describes symptoms, never claims an emergency, never gives patient details and never accepts or holds an appointment.

What is live today. Website enquiry forms only, in a small pilot. Telephone and email assessments are built but not switched on, and this notice will be updated before they are.

Whose data, and what. The assessment is of the practice, not of any person. It still touches personal data of practice staff who answer:

  • the practice's public details (name, website, published contact address, CQC register entry);
  • the name and work email address of whoever replies, and what they write;
  • screenshots of the practice's public enquiry page as we submitted it;
  • times: when the enquiry was sent and when a reply arrived;
  • a score for the practice built from those facts.

We do not seek special category data and the enquiry gives a practice no reason to send any. We refuse forms that ask for patient details, such as a date of birth or medical history.

Where the data comes from. The CQC public register, the practice's own website, and the practice's replies.

Why, and the lawful basis. To measure and report enquiry handling so practices can improve it. Adam to confirm, with legal advice: the lawful basis. The draft assessment proposes legitimate interests (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f)); it is not yet signed. Also to confirm: whether unannounced assessments proceed, and on what notice to the sector.

Use of results. A practice's results are reported to that practice. Adam to confirm: whether TMA uses an individual practice's results to offer that practice its services. Until that is decided, results are not used for marketing.

Automated activity. Enquiries are sent by software, and replies are sorted and timed by software, in future with the help of an AI model for wording that simple rules cannot read. Scores are calculated by fixed rules. A person reviews anything the software is unsure of, and a person approves every practice, every enquiry text and every batch before it is sent. Nothing here makes a decision with legal or similarly significant effect on any individual. If a member of staff sincerely asks a telephone persona whether they are speaking to a test, a recording or an AI, it says so and ends the call.

Limits we keep. At most one enquiry per channel per practice in seven days and four in thirty days for an unannounced assessment. Staff names are removed from stored reply text where our software can find them. Reports quote by role, never by name.

How to stop. Reply STOP to any message from us, or email the privacy contact above. We stop contacting that practice, cancel anything pending and keep only the record needed to make sure we do not contact it again.
