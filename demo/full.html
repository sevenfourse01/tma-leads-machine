The growth blueprint

01

Opportunity estimate

Recoverable revenue a month, split by where it leaks. Recoverable, not total: a share of every leak was never winnable by anyone, and counting it would be the flattering version of this number.

02

Twelve-month forecast

The difference against carrying on exactly as you are, month by month, as a band rather than a line. The band widens with the horizon because forecasts get less certain the further out they go. When you tell someone about this, quote the central case.

03

Recommended systems

Ranked by what each one adds on top of everything above it, not by what it would be worth on its own. Systems we would not build appear here too, with the reason, because the ones we turn down tell you more than the ones we don't.

04

Build roadmap

Two builds run at a time, each one live and earning before the next starts. The order comes out of your answers, so it changes when they do. Draft sequence, confirmed on the call

05

Goal gap

Your stated ambition against the central case, without stretching one to meet the other.
