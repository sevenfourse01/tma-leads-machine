The reason lead gen keeps failing you

Agencies sell you volume.
They don't check the maths.

Three failures turn up in nearly every agency engagement we get asked to replace. We designed the machine so none of the three can happen.

FAILURE 01

Spray and pray

Ten thousand near-identical emails from a burnt domain. It looks like activity, it reads like spam, and it torches the sending reputation you'll need for the next three years.

FAILURE 02

Personalisation theatre

"Hi {{first_name}}, loved what you're doing at {{company}}." The wrapper is personalised; the payload is a template. Buyers clocked this two years ago.

FAILURE 03

Unpriced channels

An agency puts you on paid ads because paid ads are what they sell, without once checking whether your lifetime value can carry the cost of acquisition. It usually can't.

The unglamorous part that decides everything

Deliverability, engineered
before a single send.

Most cold email fails in the DNS records, not the copy. We size and build your sending infrastructure as pure arithmetic, because getting it wrong burns the domain your business runs on. These are the 2026 Gmail and Yahoo rules, and we set them up so you don't have to.

0
Sends from your primary domain, ever.
3–5
Separate sending domains, bought and isolated
3
Mailboxes per domain, hard maximum
40
Sends per mailbox per day, ramped not rushed
SPF, DKIM and DMARC all required, with DMARC at quarantine, not the p=none that most agencies leave you on.
Three to four weeks of warmup from five sends a day, on a ramp we calculate and manage per mailbox.
Real-person mailbox patterns, never info@ or sales@, which providers filter out before a human sees them.
Custom tracking domain and List-Unsubscribe headers on every message, as the mailbox providers now demand.
Spam complaint rate held under 0.3%, the threshold above which Gmail starts throttling you without telling you.
Capacity is arithmetic, targeting is judgement. We keep the two apart: the maths to the system, the judgement to a person.

Straight answers

FAQs

Is this software I log into, or do you run it?
We run it. This is a done-for-you service, not a tool we hand you and walk away from. Our team operates the system on your behalf, stands in every loop that needs judgement, and hands you finished leads to approve. You get a dashboard to see what's happening and to approve or edit. The work is ours, and so is the accountability for it.
Then how is this different from buying an AI SDR tool?
You're hiring us to run growth for you. Software products hand you a login and a monthly bill, then leave the strategy, the deliverability, the copy and the judgement to you. We do all of that, on a system we built, with people watching it. The output can look similar; who is accountable when it goes out is the whole difference.
Do emails ever go out without a human seeing them?
No. We built it that way on purpose, and there's no setting that turns it off. The system sources, researches and drafts; then every message stops in a queue for a person to approve. Nothing can be marked approved or sent without a recorded human approver and timestamp. Once you trust it you can approve in bulk, or leave that to us. The gate stays either way.
How fast can you have this running?
An entry build lands inside a week. You get a dashboard and a direct channel within 24 hours of payment, and it's launch-ready in seven days. The full machine takes about three weeks to stand up, most of which is us reading your business rather than writing code. We'd rather take the extra fortnight than launch something that sends badly in your name.
Do I need to be technical to work with you?
No, and we won't ask you to configure anything. You describe what's costing you money in plain English; we do the engineering. The only interface you touch is a queue of drafted work with approve, edit and bin next to each item.
What happens once the system is live?
We run it and you get a plain-English report every Monday at 08:00: what came in, what got booked, what we changed, and what we're testing next. Anything we want to change waits for your approval. If a number drifts mid-week you hear it from us before you notice it.
What do you need from me to start?
Your ticket size, margin, roughly what a customer is worth over their lifetime, your monthly budget, your website, and whatever funnel numbers you genuinely have. Anything you don't know we tag as unknown rather than invent. We measure the gaps on the diagnosis call. After that, the running of it is on us.
Could a message go out that isn't true about my business?
We read your site and socials first and draft only from proof we find there. There's no mechanism for inventing a client, a case study or a percentage, and we flag anything touching a regulated claim for a separate explicit sign-off before it can even be approved.
What if the forecast says my numbers don't work?
Then we tell you, before you've paid us. It's happened: the forecast has talked us out of engagements. We'd rather lose a deal than run growth that loses you money, because the second outcome is the one you'd remember us for.
Is any of this a guaranteed return?
No. Everything we show you is a modelled range with its assumptions printed alongside it, and we log every forecast so we can score it against reality later. Anyone quoting you a single guaranteed number for lead generation is guessing and hiding it.
