The reason lead gen keeps failing you

Everyone sells you volume.
Nobody checks the maths.

Three failures show up in almost every agency engagement we're asked to replace. THE MACHINE was built specifically to make each of them structurally impossible.

FAILURE 01

Spray and pray

Ten thousand near-identical emails from a burnt domain. It looks like activity, it reads like spam, and it torches the sending reputation you'll need for the next three years.

FAILURE 02

Personalisation theatre

"Hi {{first_name}}, loved what you're doing at {{company}}." The wrapper is personalised; the payload is a template. Buyers clocked this two years ago.

FAILURE 03

Unpriced channels

An agency puts you on paid ads because paid ads are what they sell — without once checking whether your lifetime value can carry the cost of acquisition. It usually can't.

Deliverability, engineered
before a single send.

Most cold email fails in the DNS records, not the copy. We size and build your sending infrastructure as pure arithmetic — no guesswork — because getting it wrong burns the domain your business runs on. These are the 2026 Gmail and Yahoo rules, and we set them up so you don't have to.

Sends per mailbox per day, ramped not rushed
SPF, DKIM and DMARC all required — with DMARC at quarantine, not the p=none that most agencies leave you on.
Three to four weeks of warmup from five sends a day, on a ramp we calculate and manage per mailbox.
Real-person mailbox patterns — never info@ or sales@, which are filtered before a human sees them.
Custom tracking domain and List-Unsubscribe headers on every message, as the mailbox providers now demand.
Spam complaint rate held under 0.3% — the threshold above which Gmail starts silently throttling you.
Capacity is arithmetic, targeting is judgement. We keep the two apart — the maths to the system, the judgement to a person.

Straight answers

The questions we
actually get asked.

Is this software I log into, or do you run it?
We run it. This is a done-for-you service, not a tool we hand you and walk away from. Our team operates the system on your behalf, stands in every loop that needs judgement, and hands you finished leads to approve. You get a dashboard to see what's happening and to approve or edit — but the work is ours, and so is the accountability for it.
Then how is this different from buying an AI SDR tool?
You're not buying a tool — you're hiring us to run growth for you. Software products hand you a login and a monthly bill, then leave the strategy, the deliverability, the copy and the judgement to you. We do all of that, on a system we built, with people watching it. The output can look similar; who is accountable when it goes out is the whole difference.
Do emails ever go out without a human seeing them?
No — and that's deliberate, not a setting. The system sources, researches and drafts; then every message stops in a queue for a person to approve. Nothing can be marked approved or sent without a recorded human approver and timestamp. Once you trust it you can approve in bulk, or leave that to us — but the gate is always there.
What do you need from me to start?
Your ticket size, margin, roughly what a customer is worth over their lifetime, your monthly budget, your website, and whatever funnel numbers you genuinely have. Anything you don't know we tag as unknown rather than invent — and the diagnosis call is where we measure the gaps properly. After that, the running of it is on us.
Could a message go out that isn't true about my business?
We read your site and socials first and draft only from proof we find there. There's no mechanism for inventing a client, a case study or a percentage, and anything touching a regulated claim is flagged for a separate explicit sign-off before it can even be approved.
What if the forecast says my numbers don't work?
Then we tell you, before you've paid us. It's happened — the forecast has talked us out of engagements. We'd rather lose a deal than run growth that loses you money, because the second outcome is the one you'd remember us for.
Is any of this a guaranteed return?
No. Everything we show you is a modelled range with its assumptions printed alongside it, and we log every forecast so it can be scored against reality later. Anyone quoting you a single guaranteed number for lead generation is guessing and hiding it.
